## 2. Data processed

### Common data

Depending on the features used, the Services may process Discord identifiers for users, servers, channels, messages, and roles; usernames, avatars, languages, permissions, and interactions with commands, buttons, menus, forms, or reactions; and support requests and technical information required for security or diagnostics.

### TradLinker

TradLinker may process:

- messages, replies, mentions, edits, deletions, reactions, embeds, stickers, and attachments posted in connected channels;
- group, channel, language, webhook, and server-limit settings, together with mappings between original messages and their translated copies;
- poll questions, choices, images, dates, and votes, including participant identifiers and usernames;
- server Premium status and, for a subscription, Discord, customer, and subscription identifiers, payment status, and order information received from the payment provider;
- for a voluntary contribution, the Discord identifier, amount, currency, and date.

### TradAssist

TradAssist may process:

- text, messages, replies, embeds, stickers, links, and attachments selected or supplied for translation or publication;
- multilingual posts, configured emojis, and deletion delays;
- monthly counters required to apply free quotas;
- the Premium status of a server or Platinum status of a user and, for a subscription, Discord, customer, and subscription identifiers, payment status, and order information received from the payment provider.

### TradCoord

TradCoord may process:

- roles, permissions, and events relating to a member joining or being updated;
- server settings, including configured groups, languages, roles, channels, and enabled features;
- the identifier of the administrator who initiated certain operations and their date;
- welcome or coordination messages supplied by administrators;
- when enabled, the mention, identifier, and date of rule acceptance published in a channel selected by the administrator;
- the server identifier and Premium status, without receiving banking data or customer or subscription identifiers from the payment provider.

TradCoord does not create a complete permanent member list or monitor member conversations. Member information is primarily used in memory while the bot is operating.

TradLinker and TradAssist do not receive card numbers or banking details, which are handled directly by the payment provider.

Data required for a feature must be supplied in order to use it. Otherwise, the relevant Service may be unable to perform the requested command, translation, relay, vote, configuration, or subscription operation.

## 6. Retention

### TradLinker

- Member information cached in memory is kept while the configured server is active and is purged when no longer needed.
- Message mappings are kept for approximately 24 hours. Where reactions are involved, the relevant data may be kept for up to 7 days.
- Temporarily downloaded attachments are deleted at the end of the relay operation. Inactive webhook identifiers and secrets are purged after approximately 24 hours.
- Polls are kept for their duration and for up to 24 hours after closure before stored polls and votes are deleted.
- Settings are kept while the server uses TradLinker and are deleted when no longer needed.

### TradAssist

- Content and attachments submitted for translation are kept in memory only for the command or interactive session and no later than the Service's next restart. Channel translation sessions expire after approximately five minutes of inactivity.
- Translations triggered by reactions are automatically deleted after the configured delay of between 5 and 300 seconds.
- Counters, settings, access statuses, and related identifiers are kept for as long as needed for the feature, quota enforcement, abuse prevention, or dispute resolution.

### TradCoord

- Member information held in memory is kept for the session needed to operate the bot.
- Temporary security and rate-limit identifiers are deleted after the operation and no later than approximately one hour after their last use.
- Settings and related identifiers are kept while the server uses TradCoord and has the required access. They may be deleted when Premium ends, when free access closes, or upon request.

### Common periods

- Temporary payment-redirect links expire after approximately twenty minutes and can be used only once.
- Technical subscription references are kept for the subscription term and then for the time needed to handle a dispute. Accounting records and payment or contribution data are kept for up to ten years where required by law.
- Technical logs controlled by TLK Forge are kept for no more than 30 days during normal operation. Information required to investigate an incident may be kept for up to 12 months.
- Support requests are kept for up to 12 months after closure unless a legal obligation or dispute requires otherwise.
- Content, translations, polls, messages, or logs published on Discord remain subject to the choices of users or administrators and Discord's retention periods.
- Earlier versions may temporarily remain in restricted private backups. Following an erasure request, they are no longer used and are deleted or made inaccessible as soon as technically practicable.
